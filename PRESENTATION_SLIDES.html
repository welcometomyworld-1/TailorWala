What is TailorWala?

An end-to-end bespoke tailoring e-commerce platform connecting customers with master tailoring artisans for doorstep measurement visits, custom stitching, and fast delivery.

🎯

Core Purpose

To digitize the unorganized tailoring sector, eliminating inconvenient shop trips, paper sizing errors, and delayed deliveries with modern automation.

👥

Target Audience

• Customers: Doorstep bookings & tracking
• Master Tailors: Order queue & portfolio
• Staff & Admins: RBAC governance & logs

💡

Value Proposition

Guarantees flawless custom fitting without leaving home, supports local tailor economies, and ensures safety through verified ID badges.

🚪 Doorstep Measurement Consultation

Master tailors visit clients with digital tablet measurement logging.

02 • Industry Pain Points

Problem Statement & Gaps

Critical Challenges Plaguing Traditional Custom Tailoring

Slide 3 / 10
01

Inconvenient Physical Store Visits

Customers must travel 3 to 4 times per outfit (fabric drop, measuring, trial, pickup)—costing significant time, effort, and fuel in heavy city traffic.

02

Measurement & Sizing Errors

Manual paper slips and disorganized physical notebooks frequently result in lost size parameters, ill-fitting clothes, and expensive fabric rework.

03

Limited Artisan Discovery

Master tailoring artisans lack digital storefronts, portfolio showcases, or booking engines, confining their earnings to walk-in footfall.

04

Opaque Pricing & Zero Tracking

Zero real-time progress visibility, unpredictable delivery timelines, unstandardized pricing, and complete absence of formal digital receipts.

🚨 THE ROOT CAUSE: Lack of a unified digital platform connecting customers, measurement records, and master craftsmen.
